Why it works

Five skills. One exam. Your Canadian future.

Each module maps to the NCLC scale accepted by IRCC for Express Entry and provincial immigration programs.

Written Expression

Demonstrate your ability to write clearly through a personal message and an argumentative response. Evaluated on vocabulary range, grammar, coherence, and task completion.

  • Plan before writing
  • Use connectors like cependant and en revanche
  • Revise grammar agreement before submitting

Oral Expression

Speak naturally across interactive scenarios and present a point of view with fluency, pronunciation control, and lexical precision.

  • Record yourself daily
  • Use structured opinion phrases
  • Practice under strict timing

Reading Comprehension

Analyze authentic French texts and answer questions testing global meaning, details, inference, and vocabulary in context.

  • Skim before close reading
  • Spot negation and quantifiers
  • Eliminate wrong answers first

Oral Comprehension

Listen to announcements, conversations, interviews, and broadcasts. Recordings are played once, so strategy matters.

  • Listen for numbers and dates
  • Predict from the question stem
  • Take compact notes fast
